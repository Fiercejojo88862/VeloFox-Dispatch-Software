Client Booking Portal In progress

This page is reserved for the client-facing reservation form. Until it ships, ride requests can be created directly through the API.

The portal will collect

  • Pickup location — where the vehicle meets the client.
  • Drop-off location — the destination for the trip.
  • Date and time — when pickup is requested (must be in the future).
  • Vehicle class — sedan, SUV, sprinter, or limousine.
  • Passenger count — 1 to 14 guests.

Submissions go to POST /api/bookings. Dispatchers pick them up immediately on the dashboard — refresh the dashboard after adding a ride.
