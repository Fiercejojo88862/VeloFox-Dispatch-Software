The dispatch system

Every ride, end to end

VeloFox Dispatch is the logistics platform that connects clients with a Chauffeur network: instant quotes, live bookings, GPS tracking, automated payments, and Chauffeur settlement — built for the port of New York and New Jersey.

01

Booking layer

Instant quotes, saved addresses (EWR, JFK, LGA, TEB), special requests, and one-click reservation from the web.

02

Dispatch board

Live board with vehicle and Chauffeur assignments, GPS positions, and real-time conflict detection.

Four vehicle classes, automated drive-time and deadhead calculations, maintenance and compliance alerts.

Live flight and pickup monitoring, geofencing on arrival, shareable tracking links, and trip receipts.

Card vaulting, automatic wait-time and surcharge calculation, gratuities, and PDF itemized receipts.

Opt into the Chauffeur network or let partners farm trips out — with margin tracking and status updates back to them.
